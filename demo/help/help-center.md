# Sedi Help Center

Sedi is a calm workspace that runs entirely in your browser: tasks (Taskly), life goals (Boardly), time (Timely) and notes, links and files (Brainly), connected by one card system. There is no account and no server. Your cards stay on this device, and Sedi keeps working offline after your first visit.

## Feature: Cards

Every task, goal, note, event, reminder and link is the same kind of thing: a card with a title, optional details and the tool it lives in. Move a card to another tool and it takes that tool's shape: a task in Taskly, a note in Brainly, an event or reminder in Timely, a goal, task or note in Boardly. Double-click a card, or use its ⋯ Details button, to edit it.

## Feature: Getting around

The tool buttons on the right edge of the screen switch between Taskly, Boardly, Home, Timely and Brainly. A small triangle shows or hides them; on narrow windows they start hidden. On phones they're a tab bar along the bottom. The Sedi logo in the top left always goes back to Home. The menu (two lines, top right) holds your data, AI and account settings. The (?) Help button next to it opens this guide.

## Feature: Universal Board

The Universal Board is a holding area for cards in transit, opened from the dark round button in the top right. Drop cards in, switch tools, then drag them out where they belong. It also has a quick-capture field. It never scrolls: it holds as many cards as fit on screen (at least 3), shown as a count like 4/8.

## Feature: Trash and Undo

Drag any card or folder onto the bin in the bottom-right corner to delete it, or use the × on a card. Every delete shows a message with Undo for a few seconds. Clearing completed tasks and deleting lists can be undone the same way.

## Feature: Home

Home shows a large clock, today's date, the weather, and two short lists: Next up (your top To do task plus today's unfinished Timely items) and Recent activity (the last cards you changed). See all opens the full lists. Click any row to jump to that card.

## Feature: Taskly

Taskly has five columns: To do, In progress, Review, Completed and Backlog. Columns never scroll, on purpose: each holds as many cards as fit, shown as a count like 3/8 in the header. When a column fills up, finish, move or delete something. Cards in Review get a timer that brings them back to your attention. Completed never blocks you; when it overflows, its oldest cards go to the trash (with Undo).

## Feature: Boardly

Boardly is a free-form board for each part of your life, organised as category tabs (Health, Relationships, Work, Chores and Admin to start, up to 10). Place goals, tasks and notes anywhere on the board. Goals open into a SMART template. On phones the board shows as a list.

## Feature: Timely

Timely has two modes. Scheduling has Daily, Weekly and Monthly views and a Reminders board. Planning has Projects (lists of tasks) and Goals (goals broken into milestones and next actions). Timely only looks forward: you can't schedule into the past. Past one-off items move to an archive for 30 days. Today's plan in the Daily view is the same list as Taskly's To do column.

## Feature: Brainly

Brainly holds notes and folders on a free-form canvas, and links, PDFs and files in the My links column on the left. Folders can hold folders. Notes support bold, italic, headings, lists and dictation, and can be opened as a Google Doc. The search box at the top searches Brainly and the rest of Sedi.

## Feature: The assistant

The floating atom (bottom of the screen) is the assistant. Tap it and talk, or press ⌘K (Ctrl K) to type. Ask about anything you've saved, or tell it what to change, like "move passport to in progress" or "remind me to call mom tomorrow at 9am". It always asks before changing anything, and keeps a command history on this device.

## Feature: Privacy and on-device AI

By default, voice starts right away with your browser's speech recognition (in Chrome, Google processes that audio) and switches to private on-device hearing once its one-time download finishes; after that, recordings are wiped as soon as they're turned into text. To never use the browser's recognition, choose Private, on this device in Voice and hearing. Mics in cards and notes use the browser's recognition. The default AI engine runs in your browser, so nothing you type leaves your device. You can optionally add Claude or another AI service with your own key in Other AI services. API keys stay on this device and are never included in Download data.

## Glossary

- **Card** — Any task, goal, note, event, reminder or link. It lives in one tool and changes shape when moved.
- **Taskly** — Tasks in five columns that never scroll: To do, In progress, Review, Completed and Backlog.
- **Boardly** — Free-form boards for each part of your life, with goals, tasks and notes.
- **Timely** — Your time: Daily, Weekly and Monthly views, Reminders, and Projects and Goals for planning.
- **Brainly** — Notes, folders, links, PDFs and files, with search.
- **Home** — The clock, weather, Next up and Recent activity.
- **Universal Board** — The holding area for moving cards between tools.
- **Tool buttons** — The buttons on the right edge that switch tools.
- **Trash** — The bin in the bottom-right corner. Deletes can be undone from the message.
- **Review timer** — When a card in Review comes back to the top of the column.
- **SMART goal** — A goal that is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound.
- **Routine** — A Timely item that repeats. Ticking a day never deletes it.
- **Part of day** — Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Evening or Night.
- **Today's plan** — The list beside the Daily view. It is Taskly's To do column.
- **Archive** — Where past one-off Timely items wait for 30 days before deleting themselves.
- **My links** — Brainly's column for links, PDFs and files.
- **Assistant** — The atom at the bottom. Talk or type to ask about your cards or change them.
- **Private voice** — On-device speech-to-text. Recordings are wiped after transcription.
- **On-device AI** — Optional. A small AI model that runs in your browser, so the assistant can answer open questions and coach you without sending anything anywhere. Without it, the assistant still finds your cards, says what's next and makes changes you ask for.
- **Other AI services** — Use Claude or another AI service with your own key instead of the on-device AI.


## Your data: where it lives and how to keep it safe

Sedi has no accounts and no server. Everything you make is stored in this browser on this device, so your browser holds the only copy unless you save a backup.

1. Click **Open menu** in the top right.
2. Click **Your data** to see where it's stored, when you last backed up, and what can erase it.
3. Click **Download a backup** to save everything as one file. **Import a backup** restores it in any browser.

What erases Sedi: clearing "cookies and site data" (Safari: "history and website data") for this site, closing a private or incognito window, or using a different browser, profile or device. Clearing only cached images and files does not. Safari can remove data from sites you haven't opened for about a week, so on a Mac or iPhone add Sedi to your Dock or Home Screen.

Also asked as: where is my data stored; is my data safe; where does my data live; privacy of my data
